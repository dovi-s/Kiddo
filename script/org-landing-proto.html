How it works

You fund a group of kids. The money lands in real accounts and stays theirs.

01

You choose a group to fund, like your employees' kids, or every newborn in a county.

02

The money is invested in each child's own long-term account. It is permanent and unconditional.

03

You get a verified record of the impact, and the credit to tell on your own channels.

Every child gets the same thing, whoever funds it: a real account, their Memory Book, and full ownership when they come of age. Never an ad, never a pitch.
