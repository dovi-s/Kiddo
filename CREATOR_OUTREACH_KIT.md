## The 30-second demo video script (phone-recordable, no production)

Record on your phone, selfie mode or voiceover, no edits beyond a trim. The goal is "real
founder explaining the thing in 30 seconds," not a polished ad.

- **Hook (3s):** *"What if every birthday gift to your kid actually grew?"*
- **Problem (5s):** *"Grandma sends a $50 check. It's spent on Roblox by Tuesday. By the time they're 18, it's gone."*
- **Solution (10s — demo the gift flow on screen):** *"Kiddo lets anyone send a gift in 60 seconds. It goes into an investment fund, compounds for 18 years, and every gift comes with a note grandma can record. The kid sees it all at 18."*
- **Proof (6s — numbers on screen):** *"$50 a year for 18 years becomes about $1,800 at a 7% average. Compare to the check that's gone in a week."*
- **CTA (6s):** *"Link in bio to start one in 5 minutes. Free to set up. The first 1,000 families lock in $19/year forever."*

Total: 30 seconds, ~15 min to record with 3 takes.

## Outreach volume + cadence

Expect ~5% reply rate on cold creator DMs ("reach out to 100 to find 1 that works").

- **Week 1:** 100 DMs across all 4 categories (25 each). Spread over 2-3 days — IG throttles.
- **Week 2:** ONE follow-up to non-responders (more is harassment).
- **Expected:** ~5 yeses, of which ~3-4 actually film + ship.
- **Budget:** $500 = 5 × $100. If only 3 ship, the $200 underspend rolls into a 2nd batch.

## UTM tagging convention

Every tracked link follows this exact pattern so the admin dashboard attributes per-creator
conversions cleanly (the existing `signup` / `fund_created` / `gift_completed` events already
capture UTM params — no new instrumentation):

```
https://kiddofund.com/?utm_source=creator&utm_medium=video&utm_campaign=launch&utm_content={creator_handle}
```
Example: `?utm_source=creator&utm_medium=video&utm_campaign=launch&utm_content=tiktok_grandmasylvia`

- `utm_source=creator` — vs paid ads / organic / partnerships
- `utm_medium=video` — vs `bio` (static bio link) / `post` (one-shot)
- `utm_campaign=launch` — first creator campaign (future: `holiday2026`, `q1-2027`)
- `utm_content={creator_handle}` — lowercased, platform-prefixed, no spaces

## $500 test budget

- Creator payouts: **$500** (5 × $100)
- Outreach tooling: $0 (manual DMs week 1)
- Production: $0 (creators record on their own phones)
- Tracking: $0 (existing UTM + analytics + the sheet)

**Total burn: $500. Worst case: 5 ship, all flop — $500 buys data on which categories don't work.**

## When to scale (14-day measurement window)

- **0 conversions:** kill the format; talk to 5 grandmas about what would make them try.
- **1-3 / creator:** baseline working — expand to $2,500 (25 creators).
- **5+ / creator:** the format is hot — expand to $10,000 (100 creators).
- **Viral hit (one video → 100+ conversions):** scale that creator first ($500/video for the next 3), then expand to similar creators.

Kelechi's ratio ($120 → 2M views → tens of thousands in revenue) is the *upside*, not the plan.
Expect 50-200 views → 0-3 conversions per creator; the baseline decides whether to keep going.

## Refused — what NOT to do with this budget

- **No Meta/TikTok ads.** Untested format + paid traffic = double burn. Validate organic first.
- **No "kid investing" creators.** Saturated by Acorns affiliate links; you'd be ad #43 in their feed.
- **No influencer marketplaces** (AspireIQ etc.). Manual outreach to specific creators wins at this stage/budget.
- **No generic template.** Per category, per recent post — or skip the DM and pick a different creator.
- **No exclusive deals / long contracts.** Single-video tests, $100 each. Lock the relationship only after data.
- **No paid boosts** of the creator's video. Let the organic algorithm decide.
