## How it started

Emma's parents wanted one place family could send birthday gifts without juggling checks, cards, and "we'll send something later" promises.

The first version of that idea showed up around her baby shower. By the time Emma turned seven, the family already had a rhythm.

## Why they used Kiddo

They wanted the birthday ask to feel warm, not transactional. One private link felt easier than registry instructions or brokerage setup notes.

They also wanted the gifts to feel like they were building something, not just disappearing into another pile of stuff.

## What family did

- Grandparents used the gift link from the invitation
- Aunts and uncles used the same link from the group chat
- Guests at the party used the QR code on the dessert table

One link let everyone participate in the same moment without extra explanation.

## What made it feel different

The family chose a stock that felt personal to Emma's interests, which made the gift feel like more than cash.

It gave relatives a story to tell, not just a payment confirmation.
