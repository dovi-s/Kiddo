## How it started

Noah's parents were tired of answering the same question: "What do you still need?"

They wanted one answer that felt more meaningful than duplicate gifts and more useful than a check tucked into a card.

## Why they used Kiddo

The baby shower was the obvious moment. Family was already gathering. People already wanted to give. The only missing piece was one simple place to send them.

That is what the link solved.

## What happened

Family members used the link from the shower invitation, and the parents could point everyone to one destination instead of managing five different conversations.

Relatives who lived far away could participate just as easily as the guests in the room.

## What mattered most

The fund gave people a way to show up without friction, and it gave the parents the feeling that the shower gifts were building something longer-term from day one.
