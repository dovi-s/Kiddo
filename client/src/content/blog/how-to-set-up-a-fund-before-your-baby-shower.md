Here is the thing about baby shower gifts.

The onesies get outgrown in three months. The toys get forgotten in six. The gift cards get lost in a drawer.

But a gift that gets invested can still matter years later.

Here is exactly how to set up a Kiddo fund before your baby shower and make it easy for every guest to give something that lasts.

## Step 1: Create the fund before invitations go out

Do not wait until after the shower. Set it up now while the invitation, registry note, and family messages are still easy to edit.

You only need one clean destination before people start asking what to buy.

## Step 2: Choose a simple investment approach

Pick the default you want before the shower so you are not making investment decisions in a rush later.

For most families, that means either:

- a diversified S&P 500 style ETF
- a stock that feels personal to the family story
- or cash to invest later if you want to decide more slowly

## Step 3: Get your link, QR code, and gift code ready

Kiddo gives you multiple ways to share:

- the gift link for digital invitations and group chats
- the QR code for printed invites, signs, and shower tables
- the gift code for anyone who prefers typing over scanning

## Step 4: Put it directly in the invitation

You do not need a long explanation. The strongest version is simple:

> We set up a Kiddo fund for the baby, so if you want to give something that lasts, you can use this link.

That is enough.

## Why this works better than checks and cash

- Nobody has to ask where to send money.
- Gift-givers do not need an account.
- The money does not end up sitting in a drawer after the shower.
- Faraway family members can participate without friction.

## What Kiddo handles for you

- A private gift page
- Secure checkout for gift-givers
- Automatic routing into the fund
- A record of gifts and notes inside the Memory Book
