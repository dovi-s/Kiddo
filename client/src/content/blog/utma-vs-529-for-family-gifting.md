This is one of the most common questions parents ask when they start thinking about investing for their child.

UTMA or 529?

The honest answer: for many thoughtful families, both.

## Where a 529 wins

A 529 is specifically built for education savings.

- potential state tax advantages
- tax-free qualified growth for education use
- a cleaner fit when college is the primary goal

If you are primarily saving for school and you are contributing systematically yourself, a 529 deserves serious consideration.

## Where a UTMA wins

A UTMA is more flexible later.

- no education-only restriction
- simpler mental model for broader future support
- stronger fit when family gifting is part of the plan

That flexibility is the biggest reason many families still want one.

## Where Kiddo fits

Kiddo is built around a UTMA-style gifting experience because the real product problem is not just account custody. It is participation.

Families do not need a brokerage manual. They need a simple link.

That is why a Kiddo UTMA works well for birthdays, holidays, baby showers, and the moments when family wants to show up but will not navigate a complicated provider workflow.

## The practical rule

If your only goal is education-specific savings, a 529 is worth serious consideration.

If your goal is making gifting simple and giving the child more flexibility later, a UTMA is usually the better fit.

Many families use both:

- a 529 for their own systematic college saving
- Kiddo for the family-gifting side
