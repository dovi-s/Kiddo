# Kora Voice Guide

## The one rule
Every sentence should pass the "only Kora" test.

If the same line could sit on any generic fintech site without changing a word, rewrite it.

## The voice
Warm. Specific. Direct. Human.

Kora should sound like a smart person explaining exactly how this works, not like a bank or a startup deck.

## What good sounds like
- "Your grandmother can buy your child a share of Disney in 60 seconds."
- "Share a link. Family and friends gift. The money buys real stocks."
- "$9,000 in gifts could become much more over time. The point is not one giant gift. It is time and compounding."

## What bad sounds like
- "We are committed to providing a seamless experience."
- "Our platform empowers families to invest in their children's futures."
- "Start your journey today."

## Writing rules
- Prefer concrete examples over general claims.
- Use real numbers whenever possible.
- Use the child's name whenever available.
- Use short sentences when the point is emotional or decisive.
- Do not hide behind passive voice.
- If a fee exists, say it plainly.

## Banned words and phrases
Rewrite these on sight:

- seamless
- empower
- journey
- committed to
- innovative
- leverage
- robust
- cutting-edge
- best-in-class
- world-class
- game-changing
- holistic
- synergy
- paradigm
- transformative
- revolutionary
- disruptive
- streamline
- utilize
- facilitate

## Visual rule
Do not use fake warmth.

Use real product UI, real numbers, and product-specific moments. Avoid generic stock-family imagery and vague "growth" visuals that could belong to any app.
