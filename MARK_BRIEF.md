# Brand Mark Brief — Kiddo (working name)

A brief for a **designer or image-gen tool** to explore our symbol / app-icon. This is
direction, not a finished mark — generate widely, then judge with your own eye.
(Past lesson: an AI-drawn "sprout" glyph read like a weed / a $2 kids app. Avoid literal
plants. See the anti-brief below.)

---

## 1. What we are (in one line)
A place where **people invest in a child's future** — many people gift into one kid's
fund, it grows for years, and it's handed to them when they turn 18. Custodial investing,
but the product is really the *gathering of people around a child over time*.

> Name note: the name may change to a coined word. Design the mark to **stand alone as a
> symbol** (not dependent on spelling out "Kiddo"), so it survives a rename.

## 2. The core idea the mark must carry
**Gathering + growth + handing forward.** Not a single seed.

Acorns nailed the *seed* metaphor (small thing → mighty oak = spare change compounds). Ours
is a **different shape of story**: *many hands → one child → a future handed over across
years.* The mark should feel **relational and time-based**, not germinating.

Feeling target: **warm, trustworthy, grown-up, quietly optimistic.** A family you'd trust
with money — not a toy, not a bank.

## 3. Anti-brief — do NOT
- ❌ Seeds, sprouts, leaves, trees, acorns (too close to Acorns; reads "$2 kids app").
- ❌ Cartoon babies, pacifiers, blocks, rattles, googly faces (won't age with the kid; a
  17-year-old inheriting shouldn't see a toy).
- ❌ Fintech-blue, chrome gradients, generic "growth arrow" charts.
- ❌ Cupped-hands / heart-in-hands charity clichés.
- ❌ Busy detail that dies at 16px favicon size.

## 4. Style DNA + exact palette
- **Warm-geometric, slightly hand-inflected.** Crafted, not corporate-SaaS. A touch of
  imperfection is good.
- **Evergreen-first, earth-warm.** Mature and human, never candy-bright.
- **One confident form** that survives flat monochrome and tiny sizes.

Palette (our real tokens):
| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Evergreen (primary) | `#143A2C` | the mark's main body |
| Evergreen deep | `#0B2018` | shadow / depth floor |
| Cream (ground) | `#F7F3EC` | background, negative space |
| Brass gold (accent) | `#C68F30` | a single glint only — value/warmth, never the whole mark |
| Gilt highlight | `#E3B860` | optional light catch |
| Ink | `#1A1710` | monochrome version |

Rule: **gold is a highlight, not a fill.** Evergreen + cream do the work; a small brass
glint can signal "value / something precious."

## 5. Technical constraints
- Legible + recognizable at **16px** (favicon) and as an **iOS/Android app icon**.
- Works in **one flat color** (evergreen on cream, and inverted cream on evergreen).
- Square-safe (fits an app-icon safe area) and also works as a standalone glyph in a lockup.
- No text baked in — symbol and wordmark separable.

## 6. Directions to explore (ranked by my read)
1. **Rings / ripples (top pick).** Concentric rings like a tree's growth rings *or* water
   ripples — each ring = another person adding, radiating outward over years. Encodes
   *time + many contributors + growth* in one calm shape. Distinctly not an acorn; scales
   and monochromes cleanly. Could hide a subtle center (a dot = the child).
2. **Figure + arc.** An abstracted small figure (the child) with an upward/forward sweep
   behind them. "Invest in people" made literal but kept minimal so it doesn't read
   nonprofit.
3. **Gift → horizon.** A parcel/ribbon form whose top opens into an upward path or sunrise
   — the gifting act becoming a future. Riskier (can read literal), but it's our verb.
4. **Motion monogram.** A single letterform (of the final name) with a subtle upward or
   embracing gesture built into it — the safe, typographic route.

## 7. Mood / references to study
Study these for **structure and warmth**, not to copy: Acorns (single-object clarity,
what to diverge from), Mercury / Monarch (calm modern fintech geometry), Airbnb Bélo
(one ownable warm symbol), Headspace (warmth without childishness), classic monoline seals
/ growth-ring engravings (craft feel).

## 8. Paste-ready image-gen prompts (try several)
> Rings direction:
> *"Minimal brand symbol for a warm family-finance brand. Concentric rings radiating from
> a small center dot, like tree growth-rings or gentle water ripples, suggesting many
> people adding to a child's future over years. Solid deep evergreen (#143A2C) on aged
> cream (#F7F3EC), one small brass-gold glint (#C68F30). Flat, geometric, slightly
> hand-drawn, crafted not corporate. Works as a tiny app icon. No text, no leaves, no
> acorn, no gradient."*

> Figure + arc direction:
> *"Minimal, grown-up brand mark: a single abstract figure with a soft upward arc behind
> it, suggesting a child growing into their future. Deep evergreen on cream, one brass
> accent. Geometric, warm, confident, ages from child to adult. Flat, monochrome-safe app
> icon. No cartoon, no face, no charity hands."*

> Gift → horizon direction:
> *"Ownable brand symbol: a simple wrapped-gift form whose ribbon opens upward into a
> path/sunrise. Evergreen and cream, small brass glint. Minimal, warm, premium, not
> literal or cute. Flat app icon, legible at 16px. No text."*

## 9. How to judge it (the eye test)
- Does it still read at **favicon size** and in **one flat color**?
- Would a **17-year-old** inheriting the fund feel it's *theirs* — grown-up, not a toy?
- Is it **clearly not an acorn / not a sprout**?
- Does it feel **warm + trustworthy** at the same time?
- Can you describe the idea in **one sentence** ("many hands, one child, over time")? If
  not, it's decoration, not a mark.
