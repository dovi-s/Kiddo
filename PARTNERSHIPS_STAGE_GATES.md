# Partnerships Stage Gates

Updated: 2026-04-12

Purpose:
- define when each partnership tier unlocks
- keep the team from moving too early into complex channels
- tie partnerships to measurable product readiness

## Unlock Sequence

1. Consumer product
2. Gifter to parent loop
3. DriveWealth / adviser intros
4. Schools pilot
5. Employer benefits
6. Brand partnerships

Each stage only unlocks when the previous one is measurably healthy.

## Stage 0: Consumer Product

### Goal

Prove Kora works as a standalone self-serve product.

### Required Product Conditions

- parent onboarding is production-stable
- gifter checkout is stable on mobile
- first gift success path works end to end
- dashboard and activity surfaces reflect a real gift state

### Unlock Metrics

- `share_to_first_gift_rate >= 20%`
- `gift_checkout_completion >= 15%`
- `median_time_to_first_gift <= 7 days`
- `support_contact_rate on checkout-critical issues <= 5% of completed gifts`

### Unlock Output

Once this stage is healthy, the team can invest harder in the gifter loop.

## Stage 1: Gifter To Parent Loop

### Goal

Prove Kora can turn product usage into more hosts.

### Required Product Conditions

- post-gift CTA exists
- gifter email capture exists where appropriate
- attribution from gift session to new parent signup is measurable
- gifter deep link / landing experience is stable

### Unlock Metrics

- `gifter_to_parent_signup_rate >= 2%`
- `gifter_to_parent_fund_create_rate >= 1%`
- `new_parent_share_rate within 7 days >= 50%`
- `first_gift_rate for loop-generated parents >= 15%`

### Unlock Output

Once this stage is healthy, Kora has earned the right to add trust-led partnership motions.

## Stage 2: DriveWealth / Adviser Intros

### Goal

Use financial credibility to create warmer distribution and stronger trust.

### Required Product Conditions

- trust and compliance copy is consistent
- account structure and handoff explanation are clear
- Kora can explain custodial accounts simply
- there is a partnership-ready one-pager or demo flow

### Unlock Metrics

- `intro_to_meeting_rate >= 30%`
- `meeting_to_followup_rate >= 50%`
- at least `3 high-signal adviser or platform champions identified`
- at least `1 repeat referral or co-marketing path created`

### Unlock Output

Once this stage is healthy, Kora can run community pilots with stronger credibility and better institutional answers.

## Stage 3: Schools Pilot

### Goal

Prove Kora works in a trusted, local, parent-dense environment.

### Required Product Conditions

- pilot signup path exists
- school-specific landing or code flow exists
- reporting for school pilot cohorts exists
- support workflow for pilot participants exists

### Unlock Metrics

- at least `1 signed pilot champion`
- `landing_to_signup_rate >= 20%` for pilot parents
- `signup_to_fund_created_rate >= 50%`
- `fund_created_to_first_gift_rate >= 20%`
- positive qualitative signal from school champion and parents

### Unlock Output

Once this stage is healthy, Kora has evidence that trust-based group distribution works and can begin packaging for employers.

## Stage 4: Employer Benefits

### Goal

Turn Kora into a bulk parent-acquisition channel with credible packaging.

### Required Product Conditions

- employer-ready positioning exists
- landing flows can support cohort attribution
- reporting can show employer cohort outcomes
- customer support and onboarding can absorb spikes

### Unlock Metrics

- at least `1 employer design partner`
- `employee landing_to_signup_rate >= 15%`
- `signup_to_fund_created_rate >= 40%`
- `fund_created_to_first_gift_rate >= 15%`
- cost per activated fund from employer channel beats paid acquisition baseline

### Unlock Output

Once this stage is healthy, Kora has earned larger top-of-funnel attention from brands.

## Stage 5: Brand Partnerships

### Goal

Use reach to pour attention into a system that already converts.

### Required Product Conditions

- strong landing page conversion
- clear partnership CTA and offer
- reliable attribution across partnership traffic
- product can handle larger traffic spikes without degraded conversion

### Unlock Metrics

- `partnership landing_to_signup_rate >= 10%`
- `signup_to_fund_created_rate >= 35%`
- `fund_created_to_first_gift_rate >= 15%`
- partnership CAC is within target payback window
- traffic quality is stable, not vanity only

### Unlock Output

Brand distribution becomes a multiplier instead of a distraction.

## Gate Review Cadence

Review stage gates:
- monthly for strategy
- weekly during an active pilot

Each review should answer:
- did the current stage hit the unlock threshold?
- if not, is the problem product, messaging, or channel fit?
- what is the one bottleneck that must be fixed before the next stage?

## Red Flags That Mean "Not Yet"

Do not unlock the next stage if any of these are true:
- checkout is still visibly fragile
- attribution is incomplete
- support load is already too high
- product messaging changes every week
- partnership demand is coming in but conversion is poor

## Decision Rule

If the current stage is not producing a repeatable conversion path, do not escalate to the next tier.

Fix the leak before adding pressure.
