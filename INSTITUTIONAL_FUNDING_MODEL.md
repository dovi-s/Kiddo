# Institutional Funding — Model Map

*Decision-support, not a commitment. Captured 2026-07-09 from a founder ideation
thread. This is the "institutional harvest" lane: how organizations (employers,
foundations, wealthy donors, corporate CSR, local business) fund kids' accounts,
and how Kiddo earns on it. It is DOWNSTREAM of the two gates in `THE_PLAN.md`
(funded-k >= 1, then retention) and of going live. Nothing here is decided or
scheduled; it defers to THE_PLAN for sequence. Every tax/legal point needs
benefits + nonprofit counsel. Route serious moves through the advisory-panel /
compliance skills. See also `MOAT_MEMO.md` (the ASK test), `REVENUE_MODEL.md`.*

## The honest cash-flow reality (read first)

- Today revenue = $0. Nothing is live (`INVESTING_LIVE=false`).
- The consumer meter (0.10% AUM) is tiny per account and only becomes a real
  business at roughly $1B in assets. Slow by design.
- Institutional is the **best near-term REVENUE shot** (one check can beat
  thousands of consumer accounts) but it is **not a reliable near-term
  bill-payer**: it needs the product live, B2B sales cycles run months, the
  deductible version needs a 501(c)(3) built first, and checks are lumpy.
- So the real near-term problem to solve is **runway** (savings / a small raise /
  low burn), not revenue. Treat institutional money as upside that shortens the
  gap, and treat runway you control as the floor.
- Cheapest validation (do now, ~$0): a one-page pitch + a single "for
  organizations" page + a handful of real conversations. Aim for a letter of
  intent ("we will fund $X once you are live"). LOIs do not pay a bill this
  month, but they prove the money is real and are the strongest thing to put in
  front of an investor to get the runway that does.

## The two rails (every funder is one or the other)

- **Gift rail.** Money to specific, identified kids. Simple, NOT tax-deductible
  (it is a gift; gift-tax annual-exclusion rules apply). Used by employers
  funding their own employees' kids, and individuals funding kids they know.
- **Charity rail.** A Kiddo **501(c)(3)** takes a tax-deductible donation for a
  charitable CLASS of kids (foster, low-income, disaster region), and distributes
  by neutral published criteria. The donor gets the deduction and the impact and
  CANNOT pick the individual child. Used by foundations, donor-advised funds,
  UHNWI wanting a deduction, and corporate CSR.
- The tax rules for the charity rail (a charitable class, no donor-chosen
  individuals, no private benefit or control) are almost identical to the trust
  boundaries drawn independently for moat reasons. The law enforces the clean
  model.

## The revenue model (same shape for all): two fees

1. **Fee on the flow** (near-term cash). Form varies by funder.
2. **0.10% AUM tail** on the resulting balances, every year (a compounding
   annuity that outlives the front-end fee).

## Per funder (fee numbers ILLUSTRATIVE; counsel sets the real ones, esp. charity)

- **Employer (gift rail, nearest, no nonprofit needed).** "We invest in your
  kids' futures" as a benefit (flat per-child, or a match of employee
  contributions). Charge like a benefits platform: per-employee per-month, or a
  flat annual program fee, or a small percent of disbursement. Deductible to the
  employer as a benefit expense, not as charity. *Example: 200 kids x $500 =
  $100k flow; 5% admin = $5k up front, plus the tail.* Build: employer signup,
  employee enrollment, multi-account disbursement, billing.
- **Foundation (charity rail).** Grants to the 501(c)(3) for a class; you run
  eligibility, distribution, compliance, reporting; charge an admin fee
  (fiscal-sponsor / scholarship-admin range, roughly high-single to low-teens
  percent, counsel-set). *Example: $1M grant at 8% = $80k up front, plus the
  tail.* Needs the 501(c)(3).
- **Wealthy donor / DAF.** Deduction path runs through the 501(c)(3), usually out
  of their donor-advised fund (a large existing pool). Known-kids path is a plain
  large gift on the gift rail (tail only, maybe a small concierge fee).
- **Corporate CSR / brand.** Same as a foundation (via the 501(c)(3), admin
  percent plus tail), possibly a larger program/reporting fee (they pay for the
  verified-impact dashboard and receipt they use in ESG). Clean version only, see
  the locks.
- **Local business.** Gift rail, small, self-serve: a flat program fee plus the
  tail. *Example: a dentist funds every kindergartener in town, $25 each.*

## Two strategic refinements (from the thread)

- **Prefer sponsors who DISTRIBUTE over sponsors who only fund.** An employer to
  its employees, a local business to its town, a foundation to its cohort brings
  you families (real acquisition and retention). Pure brand goodwill only
  harvests the audience you already have. Bias hard toward distributors; treat
  pure goodwill as a quiet revenue line, not a growth engine. This is a
  distribution channel, NOT a network effect; the moat stays counter-positioning
  plus owned from-birth demand.
- **The clean model self-limits to CSR, benefits, and employer-branding budgets,
  not ad budgets, and that ceiling is the safety.** Ad budgets buy attention,
  targeting, and a message, which is the poison. Any plan to grow past the clean
  ceiling is a plan to reintroduce advertising to kids. The bound is what keeps it
  honest.

## The clean-line locks (the ASK / Memory Book test)

- Money is permanent and unconditional. Never contingent on a purchase, view,
  click, or share.
- Target COHORTS and CAUSES, never individual children (charity law plus
  creepiness).
- NO brand-authored message inside the fund. Any note is cause-authored (about
  the kid or the reason, never a brand slogan). "Dream big, Nike" in the Memory
  Book is the refused shape.
- On-platform attribution is a neutral factual line. The company's reputation and
  badge accrue OFF-platform (careers page, ESG report, LinkedIn). No prominent
  in-app brand leaderboard, which is ad inventory with a nicer name.
- Litmus: could this render in the Memory Book at 18 as "[Entity] gave [Amount]
  to [Kid] because [Reason]" and read as true and warm? If it needs an affordance
  a loving relative would not get, it is out.
