# Kiddo for Organizations — outreach one-pager

*Draft for the founder's validation conversations. NOT public. Use it as a call
script, a short email, or the skeleton of a deck. Honest framing: we are not live
yet, so the ask is interest and a soft "would you fund this," not a sale. Pairs
with `INSTITUTIONAL_FUNDING_MODEL.md`.*

## The one line

Kiddo lets an organization put permanent, invested dollars into children's futures.
The money becomes the child's own long-term investment account. No ads, no strings,
and the organization gets the credit for real impact.

## Why an organization does this

- **Employer.** A benefit your people actually feel. You invest in your employees'
  kids' futures. It attracts and keeps talent, and almost no one else offers it.
- **Foundation or donor.** Fund a group of kids who need a head start, for example
  every foster youth in a county. Verified, auditable impact, and through our
  charitable arm it can be tax-deductible.
- **Local business.** Fund every kid in your town or school. Real local goodwill
  people remember, for the cost of what you would spend on ads.

## What the organization gets

- A real, verifiable line of impact ("we funded N kids, $X, invested for their
  futures").
- Credit you can use on your own careers page, ESG report, and press. The
  reputation is yours to tell.
- For the charitable path, a tax-deductible receipt.

## What the child gets

- A real investment account with money that is theirs, permanently.
- The same experience every family gets: their account, their Memory Book, and
  full ownership when they come of age.
- Never an ad, never a pitch. Just the money, and if the funder leaves a note, a
  warm one about them, not a brand slogan.

## The honest ask (the whole point of the call)

We are not live yet. I am not selling you anything today. I want to know two things:

1. Would your organization fund kids' futures this way?
2. If yes, roughly how much, and which group of kids?

If it is a fit, I will send a short letter of intent so you are first in line when
we launch.
