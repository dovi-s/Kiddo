## The razor (read before adding an entry)

1. **If the kid voiced the lesson, it is fake.** Real kids resolve confusion
   and move on. The "ohhh" is internal and usually never said out loud. "So
   everybody built it together?" is an adult's thesis in a kid costume. The
   tell is *who does the concluding*. If the kid delivers the meaning, you
   wrote it.
2. **Keep the friction and the selfishness.** The realest money-questions are
   unsentimental: "why can't I buy a toy with it?", "who took it?" after a down
   month, "I didn't do anything" after an up one. Do not sand the friction off.
   Warmth is the cheese; friction is the signal.
3. **Authority is the real data only.** If the moment could not have come from
   looking at the actual account, gifts, or timeline, it does not belong here.
4. **Record verbatim. No cleanup. No lesson attached.** Write what was said,
   not what it meant. The meaning is found later, across many entries, not
   asserted per entry.
