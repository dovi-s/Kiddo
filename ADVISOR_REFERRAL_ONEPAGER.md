## What Kiddo is

A custodial investing app built around **gifting a child's future.** Family and friends
gift into a child's investment account (UTMA / personal) at birthdays, holidays, and
milestones — real fractional shares of companies the kid knows, growing toward 18. Every
gift becomes a permanent **Memory Book** of who showed up and what they wrote.

## Why it's complementary to your practice — not competitive

**You manage the parents' wealth. Kiddo does the part you don't:** the gift-occasion kids'
accounts, the grandparent / aunt / godparent who wants to give, and the relationship and
record around it. You keep the AUM and the client; their family gets a premium, tax-aware
way to handle the gifting you already field questions about.

## The questions your clients already ask you — Kiddo is the clean answer

- *"What do I give my grandkid that isn't another gift card?"* → real shares, growing to
  18, with their name on the family's app — a gift that compounds, not one that's spent.
- *"How do I do this without the gift-tax mess?"* → gifts are sized under the **annual
  exclusion** by design; above it, the **Form 709** reporting is the client's standard
  filing (you'd handle it as you would any gift). Kiddie-tax applies to distributions,
  same as any UTMA — nothing new for you.
- *"Is it real / safe?"* → custody, monthly statements, and tax reporting sit with a
  **registered, regulated broker-dealer partner (Member FINRA / SIPC).** Kiddo is the
  software + experience layer; **we carry the securities / custody / COPPA compliance** —
  not you, not your client.

## What a referral actually looks like (low-touch)

You mention Kiddo when a client asks about gifting to a kid or grandkid, or you hand them
this sheet. That's it — no portal to manage, no integration, no fiduciary exposure on you.
*(Optional, when it's built: a tracked referral link so the intro is attributed to you.)*

## Why it fits a long-horizon practice

The money compounds for ~18 years and **hands off to the child as an adult** — the exact
multi-generational arc you advise on. The first time a family invests for a child is the
first time they think about the next generation. Kiddo is built for that moment, and your
introduction is what makes it trusted.
