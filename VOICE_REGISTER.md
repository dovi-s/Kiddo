# Voice register: invest in people

The product is a place where people invest in a child's future, with money and
with words. The voice should sound like that act is serious and loving, not cute.
Treat the adult as a capable person doing something that matters. Keep the warmth;
lose the coo.

Founder-owned: this is brand voice. Propose changes, never slip them in. The
existing locks still hold (no em-dashes, no Sparkles, "gifter" not "giver").

## The test
Read the line out loud to a grandparent who just moved $250. If it sounds like a
greeting card, or like a kids' app talking up to them, it is wrong. If it states
what happened and why it matters, it is right.

## The 7 tells, and the fix
1. Growth as a living thing ("watch it grow", "growing", the sprout emoji). Fix:
   say what they own and what it is worth. Name the asset, not the wonder.
2. Diminutives ("a little more", "a tiny piece", "dream up"). Fix: drop the
   shrink. "Add more each month." "A real share." "Start the fund."
3. Canned celebration ("You're all set!", trailing exclamations). Fix: state the
   result and the next step. "Verified. Here is the dashboard."
4. Sentiment inflation ("means more than you know", "people who love", the same
   "showed up" three times in one card). Fix: name the act once, concretely. The
   specific dollar and the specific person land harder than the feeling.
5. Emoji next to money. Fix: never. The number stands on its own.
6. Diminutive warmth ("a warm note", "a warm welcome"). Fix: say what actually
   happens. "We'll let the parents know."
7. Heirloom overclaim ("the whole story", "handed down", "not a cold paperwork
   event"). Fix: show it instead of insisting on it. "A record of every gift and
   who gave it."

## Keep
The love is the moat. The Memory Book, the people behind the money, the at-18
handoff are the point. Mature the register so the love reads heavier, not so it
disappears.
