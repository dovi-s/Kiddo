Add these to make it “20 years perfected” and remove the stuff people hate
1) The single most important rule

Never make the contributor think.
One default button: Contribute to their Future Fund.
Everything else is a secondary link.

If you do only one thing right, do this.

Product improvements that remove friction and complaints
2) “Explain it in one sentence” everywhere

Every page needs a one-line explainer that changes by context.

Examples

Moment page: “A modern way to give a gift that lasts.”

Checkout: “Your contribution becomes a long-term fund, managed by the family.”

Receipt: “You contributed $180. It will be invested into the Future Fund by end of day.”

No jargon. No “portfolio” unless behind “Learn more.”

3) Remove the awkwardness with tasteful defaults

People hate anything that feels like “donate to my kid.”

Make it feel like premium etiquette:

“If you were thinking of giving a gift, this is the easiest way.”

“No gifts required. If you want to contribute, it takes under a minute.”

Add a setting in the Moment editor:

Tone: “Celebration” vs “Low-key”
This changes copy automatically.

4) The “Trust strip” (conversion-critical)

A small strip under the main CTA on public pages:

“No account required”

“Pay with Apple Pay”

“Secure checkout”

“Clear receipt”

Then a “How it works” drawer:

Who owns it

When it invests

Refund policy (simple)

Support

People need legitimacy in 10 seconds.

5) Make timing and status bulletproof

This is where finance products feel sketchy.

Every contribution has visible status with plain language:

Paid

Pending investment (invests by end of day)

Invested

Held as Seed

If you batch trades, you must commit to a simple SLA and show it everywhere.

6) Introduce “Seed” as the universal failsafe

Seed solves:

brokerage KYC delays

user indecision

“I want to ask later”

operational risk

But do not let Seed become “cash registry.”
If Seed is selected:

show a clear “Investing rule” (default: auto-invest into Future Fund after 14 days unless guardian changes)

7) Goal cards are the social engine

Every Moment gets an optional Goal Card by default. This is what people share in group texts.

Goal Card includes:

goal title (College head start, First car, Future Fund)

progress bar

“Share to group chat” button

contributor list optional

This converts indecision into coordination.

8) Thank-you system that people actually love

This is a major reason people abandon registries.

Make it feel like magic:

auto draft thank-yous from the contributor message

one-click send

“send all” option

printable export for handwritten notes

optional short video thank-you

Also: show “Thank-you completion” as a progress bar. People like finishing.

Brand and UX that feels like a real category leader
9) Card-first system (the core artifact)

Your product is the card, not the dashboard.

Requirements

Card reveal after checkout

“Add to Apple Wallet” later

Print fold card that looks like stationery

Shareable link with the card view

Every share should look expensive.

10) Premium themes without being “cute”

Theme system must be restrained:

neutral base

subtle accent

no gradients that scream startup

no illustrations that feel childish

Themes should feel like:

high-end wedding invitation

premium stationery

modern museum catalog

11) Copy rules that prevent cringe

Hard rules for all public copy:

no hype

no “build wealth”

no return talk

no finance jargon

no guilt language

Preferred words:

gift, contribute, head start, future, milestone, in their corner

Avoid:

invest now, outperform, growth, guaranteed, passive income

Advanced features that increase conversion without complexity
12) Smart amount presets by template

People hate thinking about “how much is normal.”

Each template has culturally neutral defaults plus optional “customize.”

Examples

Birthday: 25, 50, 100, 150

Wedding: 100, 150, 250, 500

Bar mitzvah: 54, 100, 180, 360 (only when that template is chosen)

Also allow “Host recommended” badge.

13) Two privacy modes

People hate public money.

Privacy controls:

Public: show names only, hide amounts

Private: show nothing publicly, only host sees details

Contributor option: “hide my name”

Default should be conservative.

14) Receipts that remove anxiety

The receipt should be extremely clear and professional:

amount

fee

net amount

where it goes (Future Fund or Seed)

when it invests

refund policy link

support link

This reduces chargebacks.

15) A unified “moment builder” that is fast

Make Moment creation feel like Notion, not a bank form.

Moment builder features:

live preview

template switcher

theme switcher

share assets auto-generated

OG preview auto-generated

QR download

print-ready PDF

16) Performance is part of the brand

Targets:

Public page loads under 1.5s on mobile

Checkout ready under 1s after clicking contribute

Card reveal under 1s after payment success

A slow gift checkout kills conversion.

The “20-year perfected” failure-proofing
17) The top complaints you must design out

“I don’t get it”
Fix: one sentence explainer + default CTA.

“Do I need an account”
Fix: no contributor accounts, trust strip.

“Where did my money go”
Fix: status timeline + receipt clarity.

“This feels like asking for donations”
Fix: tone modes + tasteful copy.

“I forgot to send thank-yous”
Fix: thank-you center + reminders + drafts.

“Fees feel sneaky”
Fix: simple service fee and drawer.

“Why is it complicated to share”
Fix: QR + link + print + wallet in one share drawer.

“It looks like a finance app”
Fix: card-first, memory-book recipient view.

“Perfected” conversion layout for the public Moment page

Above the fold, mobile-first:

Photo + name

Headline: “A gift they will still have in 10 years”

One-liner explainer

Primary CTA: “Contribute”

Trust strip (no account, Apple Pay, secure, receipt)

Preset amounts grid

Optional recurring toggle (collapsed by default)

Message preview (what the card will look like)

Then below:

Goal card

Messages preview (if enabled)

How it works drawer

Support link

Footer

Add one big “this becomes a platform” layer (without clutter)
18) Household becomes the long-term system

Once a family creates Everleaf, they should never leave.

Household features:

multiple profiles

one permanent link per profile

many Moment pages per profile

recurring contributors

annual recap email (“Ari’s year in gifts”)

milestones

That is the retention and multi-year compounding.

Final “builder-ready” additions to your spec

Add these sections to your handoff document:

Design tokens (colors, type scales, radius, shadows, spacing)

Component library list (buttons, cards, drawers, toast, skeleton, amount picker, share drawer, trust strip)

Public page performance budget (bundle size targets, image rules, caching)

Copy library (headlines, one-liners, drawer text, error states)

State machine diagrams for Contribution and Brokerage statuses

Template JSON schema defining presets, default goal, theme options, tone mode
