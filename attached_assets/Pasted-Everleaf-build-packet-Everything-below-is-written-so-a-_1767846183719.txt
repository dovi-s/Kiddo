Product definition
What Everleaf is

Everleaf is a gifting layer for long term assets, delivered through beautiful shareable pages.

People do not “send money.” They contribute to a recipient’s fund through a Moment Page (birthday, bar mitzvah, graduation, wedding, baby, etc). The fund is backed by a real account (embedded brokerage partner) and can auto-invest into a simple default option.

Two primary entry products

Always-on funds
A permanent giving link for a child or individual (works year-round).

Moment Pages
Event-specific pages that feel like premium registry pages and create natural sharing.

Both map to the same underlying fund(s).

Refund rules

Before trade execution, full refund possible

After execution, refund becomes complex and should be handled as policy, likely not offered in most cases except fraud or error
This must be reflected in disclosures and in how quickly you batch trades.
