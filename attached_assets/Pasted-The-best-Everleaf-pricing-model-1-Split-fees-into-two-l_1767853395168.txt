The best Everleaf pricing model
1) Split fees into two lines everywhere

At checkout, show two explicit line items:

Card processing (pass-through, at cost)

Everleaf service fee (your revenue)

This solves the “are we making money?” confusion and builds trust.

2) The core pricing engine
A) Giver-paid (default, best conversion)
Card / Apple Pay / Google Pay

Card processing: pass-through (what Stripe charges you)

Everleaf service fee: 2.0% (cap $12)

Bank transfer (ACH)

Bank transfer fee: small flat pass-through (or $0 if you subsidize later)

Everleaf service fee: 1.0% (cap $12)

Why this is the best default:

Simple and transparent

You are profitable on the core action (gifting)

You naturally push larger gifts to ACH without forcing it

B) Fee-free gifting (host chooses to cover)

When “Fee-free for guests” is ON:

Guest sees $0 fees

Host pays:

processing at cost

Everleaf service fee depends on plan and caps (below)

This is what hosts actually want for weddings and big events.

3) Host plans that people understand and love
Free

$0

Unlimited funds (Always-on Family Fund)

Unlimited event pages (Birthday, Bar Mitzvah, Graduation, Wedding)

Templates, QR, gift page, basic thank-you list

Default: guests pay fees

Optional: host can cover fees on a per-event basis (but no discounts)

Free is your viral engine. It is also profitable because giver-paid gifts include your service fee.

Plus (per event)

$99 per event
For one big milestone where hosts want a premium feel and fee-free guests.

Includes:

Fee-free for guests toggle

Premium templates + matching thank-you cards

Address capture

Auto thank-you drafts

Privacy controls (hide amounts publicly)

Economics when fee-free is ON:

Everleaf service fee waived on first $5,000 of gift volume

After $5,000: 1.0% Everleaf service fee

Host pays processing at cost the whole time

Why this is the right shape:

Hosts feel they “bought” the fee-free experience

Your downside is capped

Large events still generate margin

Family (annual household)

$199 per year
For families with multiple kids and years of events.

Includes:

Up to 10 events/year

Household dashboard (multiple kids)

Recurring gifting management (grandparents love this)

Everything in Plus

Economics when fee-free is ON:

Everleaf service fee waived on first $10,000/year gift volume

After $10,000: 1.0% Everleaf service fee

Host pays processing at cost

Why Family is the $5B move:

It turns “a one-time registry” into “the default gifting layer for life”

4) Recurring gifts: the compounding machine

Give recurring a small incentive that feels generous:

If a gift is recurring, reduce Everleaf service fee by 0.5%

Card recurring: 2.0% becomes 1.5%

ACH recurring: 1.0% becomes 0.5%

You will make more money long-term because recurring retention is the real moat.

5) The “cashapp for stocks” idea: keep it out of v1

Do not add peer-to-peer “pay friends in stock” early. It adds:

heavy compliance edge cases

fraud and chargebacks

muddled positioning

Everleaf wins by being the gifting and life-event layer, not general payments.

6) Brokerage economics: treat as upside, not survival

Do not build a model that requires brokerage revenue share to survive.

Assume brokerage economics are upside:

cash sweep revenue share

referral or program revenue

long-term AUM-linked take

Great if you get it. Never rely on it early.

7) The missing piece that makes this feel premium: “Gift Artifacts”

People do not love finance. They love the gift feeling.

Include:

beautiful digital card reveal (always)

printable folded card template (always)

optional physical keepsake upsells (high-margin):

Printed card mailed: $7.99

Framed certificate: $29

“Memory capsule” (photos + notes timeline): $19/event

These do not hurt conversion because they happen after checkout.

8) How this stays profitable at scale

Your cost drivers at scale are:

payments disputes and fraud

support

brokerage program minimums and servicing costs

trade execution and batching logic

This model protects you because:

Free gifts always carry a service fee

Fee-free gifts shift payment costs to hosts

Waiver caps prevent runaway costs on paid plans

Large events still generate service fee above caps

9) The pricing page copy structure (clean, non-fintech)

Use three cards, one sentence each:

Free
Create a fund in minutes. Guests cover fees.

Plus
$99 per event. Make gifting fee-free for guests.

Family
$199 per year. Multiple kids and milestones, one home base.

Then one plain-language drawer:
“How fees work”

Card processing is pass-through.

Everleaf service fee powers the platform, support, and gifting features.

Hosts can choose fee-free for guests on Plus or Family.
