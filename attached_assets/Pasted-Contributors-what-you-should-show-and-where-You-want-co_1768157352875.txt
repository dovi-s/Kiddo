Contributors: what you should show and where

You want contributors visible at three levels, with privacy controls.

1) Fund-level contributors

Purpose: “who is in this kid’s corner overall”
Show:

Total contributors (unique people)

Top recent contributors

Lifetime contributions by person (optional toggle)

Notes/messages (scrollable feed)
Controls:

“Show contributor names on public page?”: Yes/No

“Show amounts publicly?”: Yes/No

“Allow anonymous gifts?”: Yes/No

2) Event-level contributors (most important)

Purpose: thank-yous, social proof, and post-event admin
Show:

Event total

Contributor list for that event only

Status per gift: Pending / Invested / Failed / Refunded

Message + name + email (if they consent) + address collection (optional)
Actions:

“Send thank-yous” (bulk)

“Export CSV”

“Resend receipt”

“Refund” (if needed, with policy constraints)

3) Gift-level receipt details

Purpose: trust
Show:

Amount gifted

What it bought (or what it will buy)

Timestamp

Transaction ID

Investment status

Public pages should be “tasteful”. Private dashboard can be “complete”.

Acorns style “invest for you”: is that the plan?

No, not in the Acorns sense.

What you are building

Everleaf is a gifting and registry product where:

Gifts come in

They invest into a default basket or a chosen stock

The host controls defaults and rules

What you are not building (in v1)

A round-ups product

“Spend money and we invest the change”

A general robo-advisor replacement

You can later add “recurring gifts” that feel similar (monthly, birthday, holidays), but it is still gifting, not round-ups.

You want “Pick” not hidden behind Advanced

Agreed. You can keep it prominent without blowing up conversion if you structure the page correctly.

The best gifting screen layout

Above the fold, two equal primary choices:

Option A: Gift to their Future Fund (recommended)

One tap

Simple sentence: “A diversified fund chosen by the family.”

Option B: Pick a stock (your choice)

Search box with typeahead

Also show quick chips: Apple, Disney, Tesla, VOO, SPY, VT, etc

This satisfies “I want to pick” while still giving nervous users a safe default.

Prevent decision overload without hiding “Pick”

Add a “Favorites” shelf that the host sets per Fund or per Event:

“Mila’s favorites” (5 to 10 picks max)

Gift-giver can choose from logos instead of searching tickers
This keeps “pick” simple and fun.

What actually happens when someone picks a stock

Gift flow should be deterministic:

Giver selects: $100 + Disney

Payment captured

Gift shows as Pending

At next execution window, Everleaf places a buy

Receipt updates to: “Invested into DIS at executed price”

Holding appears in “Where it’s invested” at the Fund level

If the market is closed, show: “Will invest when markets open.”

Where “where it’s invested” belongs

Holdings are always at the Fund level (because that is the brokerage account).

Events are attribution layers (how the gift arrived), not separate portfolios.

So yes:

“Where it’s invested” = Fund

“Contributions and notes” = Fund and Event

“Event totals” = Event

“Holdings” should not fragment by event unless you are doing sub-accounts (do not do that in v1)

The one rule that keeps this understandable

One Fund equals one brokerage account.
Events are pages under the Fund.

If you later support multiple brokerage accounts per person (taxable + IRA), treat those as separate Funds:

“Dovi Fund (Taxable)”

“Dovi Retirement (IRA)” (optional later, and complicated)
Do not mix them inside one Fund.

Minimum controls you need for “Pick” to be safe and clean

Host settings per Fund:

Allow picking individual stocks: On/Off

If On, limit to:

Stocks and ETFs only

No leveraged ETFs

No options, crypto, OTC, penny stocks

Max number of holdings (soft limit): ex 25

Rebalancing behavior: none in v1 (do not do robo allocation)

This avoids a messy portfolio while still letting people pick.
