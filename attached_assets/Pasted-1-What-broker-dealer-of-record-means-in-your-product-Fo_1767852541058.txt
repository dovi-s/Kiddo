1) What “broker-dealer of record” means in your product

For an Everleaf-created investing account:

Broker-dealer of record (BD): the entity responsible for brokerage services, KYC/AML, trading permissions, regulatory obligations.

Clearing/custody: the entity that holds customer assets, clears trades, issues statements and tax forms (often the same as the BD in clearing models).

Everleaf: the product layer, not the BD (in the model you want).

Users connecting to Schwab/Fidelity is a different product path (and should be optional later).

2) The best setup for Everleaf (lock this in)
Default: Embedded account powered by an Apex-based program (B)

This is the “best” for conversion and UX.

You pick one of these program structures:

Option 1: Apex is both BD + clearing/custody

Apex Clearing Corporation is the BD of record and also clearing/custody.

Your app copy can say brokerage services are provided by Apex, clearing/custody by Apex.

This is the simplest vendor graph.

Option 2: Introducing broker-dealer + Apex clearing (common too)

A separate introducing BD is the broker of record.

Apex clears/custodies.

More parties, sometimes better feature flexibility, sometimes better support structures.

Which is “best” depends on: minors support, account types, APIs, minimums, and operational burden. But for you, the key is: pick one embedded stack and standardize the default experience.

3) Should people be able to connect “the obvious brokers”?

Eventually, yes, but not as the core.

Why “connect Schwab/Fidelity” as the default is a mistake

Account linking + verification adds friction

Transfers are slow and fail often

It breaks instant “gift card reveal” and immediate confirmation

It increases support volume and disputes

The right version of “connect” (C) later

Add “Advanced: consolidate”:

Transfer your Everleaf holdings out to Schwab/Fidelity (ACATS)

Or transfer an external account into Everleaf (ACATS)
That gives power users an escape hatch without destroying your conversion funnel.

4) What you should decide now (minimal, critical decisions)

If you want a $5B company, decide these 4 things and build everything around them:

Default rail: Embedded account inside Everleaf (yes).

Account types you support day 1: Adult + custodial for minors (UGMA/UTMA style).

Asset menu day 1: Basket-first (Future Fund), Education option, Favorites optional.

Connect external brokers day 1: No (only later as transfer/consolidation).

5) “What’s best?” Decision rule

Pick the embedded brokerage program that gives you:

fast KYC that works well on mobile

custodial/minor accounts support

fractional shares + ETFs

predictable pricing (no brutal minimums) or minimums you can afford

clean API for account status + holdings + activity

responsive support escalation path

The “best” is the one that lets you ship a seamless flow and not get killed by minimums/support.

6) What you need to do next

You need to pick the default structure and then your copy and pricing become real.

If you’re still unsure, lock the product stance anyway:

Everleaf creates a new account by default.

External brokerage connections come later and are framed as transfers.

Then you evaluate partners based on the decision rule above.
