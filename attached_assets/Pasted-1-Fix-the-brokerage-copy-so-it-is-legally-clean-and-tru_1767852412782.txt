1) Fix the brokerage copy so it is legally clean and trust-building

Your current line implies Everleaf is the brokerage. Replace with:

“Open an Everleaf investing account.”

“Brokerage services provided by [Broker-Dealer Legal Name], Member FINRA/SIPC.”

“Clearing and custody provided by Apex Clearing Corporation, Member FINRA/SIPC.”

“SIPC protects against brokerage failure, not market losses (up to $500k including $250k cash).”

Do not use “SIPC insured” alone.

2) Lock the product architecture: B as default, C as later transfer

Default: embedded account inside Everleaf for the recipient (kid custodial or adult).

Later: “Already have a brokerage?” as transfer/consolidate (ACATS), not “gift directly into Schwab.”

3) Pricing must be recut to include non-Stripe costs

You need a model that explicitly budgets for:

brokerage/clearing program fees + minimums

KYC/IDV costs

ACH costs/returns

chargebacks/fraud loss reserve

support per account and per event

If you do not model broker minimums, you can be “profitable on paper” and still lose money.

4) Change plan positioning to remove confusion about who pays fees

Add a clear toggle at event creation:

Default (Free): guests pay processing

Optional (Host-paid): host can cover fees per gift (but requires Plus/Family or per-event add-on)

This reduces friction and makes “Plus” feel instantly valuable.

5) Add one missing product primitive: “Always-on Fund” + “Event Pages”

Your structure should be:

Household account

Child Fund (always-on link)

Event pages (Bar mitzvah, Birthday, Graduation) that all route into the same fund

Adult Fund (optional)

This matches how families actually behave and avoids “new account per event.”

6) Make “Seed mode” operationally real

Define one rule and ship it:

“Seed gifts auto-invest into the default basket after X days unless parent changes it.”

If Seed can sit forever, you create support and compliance headaches.

7) Add a single “trust drawer” on every gifting page

One expandable section with:

where money sits before investing

when it invests

who controls it (parent for minors)

refunds policy (before trade vs after trade)

fees shown plainly

This will lift conversion and reduce support.

8) Define “refund and dispute logic” up front

You need deterministic rules:

before trade executes: refundable

after trade executes: refund becomes sell + settle (or not allowed)
Also set a fraud/chargeback reserve in the model.

9) Track one activation metric (“7 friends in 10 days” equivalent)

Pick one and instrument it:

“Event created → 3 gifts received within 72 hours”
or

“First gift completed in under 60 seconds”
This becomes your PMF signal.

10) Decide your “asset menu” defaults

Do not ship a long list.

Default: Future Fund (broad index style basket)

Option: Education (529 or education-themed basket)

Option: Favorites (optional, secondary)
Keep tickers hidden behind “advanced.”
