What’s subtly overbuilt or unclear

Invested value (what’s in the market)

Pending gifts (not invested yet)

Recommendation

The “holy shit I can gift them a TSLA share with Apple Pay” idea

Agree with the feeling. It is a huge hook.

But for MVP, do it in a way that does not explode complexity:

MVP-safe version of that delight

Giver chooses:

Optional “in honor of 5th birthday”

You show a fun line like:

Under the hood:

It’s a cash contribution

The fund’s strategy buys according to preset rules

Later version (harder)

True “gift 1 share of TSLA”

More edge cases: fractional rules, price movement, market hours, trade timing, cancellations, what happens if the trade fails, suitability, disclosures.

So yes to the “TSLA share” moment, but ship it as an estimated outcome from a dollar gift first.

Brokerage / investing / transactions in an MVP

This is the highest-risk area to overdo.

The clean MVP approach

Before activation (KYC not done)

Allow “pledges” only, or allow gifts that sit as pending until activation.

Your screenshot already hints at this. That is good.

After activation

Gifts become cash in the brokerage account.

A simple rule invests them:

“Invest next market open”

Activity timeline should be explicit:

Received → Processing → Invested → Settled

Be obsessive about clarity

People will forgive fewer features.

They will not forgive confusion with money.
