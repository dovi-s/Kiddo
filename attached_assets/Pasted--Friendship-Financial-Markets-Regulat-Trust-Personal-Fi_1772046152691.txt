Whose name will be on the account? This is who will be receiving the tax consequences.

If you put the account in your name, you run the risk of having to pay tax on the transfer of funds as income. You also will have a 1099 sent to the IRS for gains/losses in your name. Getting them to allow you to not recognize that is not easy. Would she incur higher or lower taxes?

If you create the account in her name, you might have problems at the broker dealer. They might be required to deduct taxes on gains/losses and remit those directly to her government. She can always designate you as authorized to do her transactions, and there would be little consequence. If you do not have such authority, then you run the risk of her claiming you created losses for her. Without an authorization, the SEC could claim you are acting as an unregistered advisor. Not likely, but possible.

In other words. be very careful about what you are doing. It’s not illegal, but you will have to follow the taxation rules.

Yes. You do have to have that person’s permission of course.
