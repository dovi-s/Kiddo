Greenlight Logo
Greenlight
Greenlight aims to improve financial literacy for families. It offers a platform that allows parents to teach their children about money management through earning, saving, spending, giving, and investing. It includes features for spending controls, chore management, allowances, and investments for kids. It serves the financial service sector. Greenlight was formerly known as Greenlight Me. It was founded in 2014 and is based in Atlanta, Georgia.

Goalsetter Logo
Goalsetter
Goalsetter is a financial technology company that provides a goal-based savings and gifting platform for children. The company offers a debit card that includes financial education through quizzes and a platform for investing in fractional shares of ETFs and stocks. Goalsetter serves families who aim to teach their children about money management and investing. Goalsetter was formerly known as Sow. It was founded in 2015 and is based in Evansville, Indiana.

Stash Logo
Stash
Stash is a personal finance app that offers a platform for budgeting, investing, and saving for retirement. The app includes features such as a robo-advisor service and stock rewards for purchases. Stash also has a workplace benefit program, StashWorks, which is designed to support financial wellness for employees. Stash was formerly known as Collective Returs. It was founded in 2015 and is based in New York, New York.

Acorns Logo
Acorns
Acorns is a financial technology company that provides savings and investment services within the fintech sector. The company offers products that enable individuals to save and invest their spare change, plan for retirement through individual retirement accounts, and invest for children with custodial accounts. Acorns serves the personal finance and investment sectors, aiming to assist Americans with financial management. It was founded in 2012 and is based in Irvine, California.

Stockpile Logo
Stockpile
Stockpile is a financial technology company that provides investment and financial education services for families. The company offers a platform where families can save, invest in stocks and ETFs, and manage spending. Stockpile serves the family financial education and investment sector. It was founded in 2010 and is based in Glendale, California.

UNest Logo
UNest
UNest is a company that provides custodial investment accounts for children within the financial services sector. The company offers investment advisory services, a program for children's accounts, options for family and friends to contribute, and term life insurance. UNest serves parents who wish to save and invest for their children's future. It was founded in 2019 and is based in North Hollywood, California.
