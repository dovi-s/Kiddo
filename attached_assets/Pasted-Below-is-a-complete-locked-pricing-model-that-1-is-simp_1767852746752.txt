Below is a complete, locked pricing model that (1) is simple enough to convert, (2) stays fair to users, and (3) is designed to stay margin-positive even with brokerage stack costs.

Everleaf pricing model
A) Core primitives

Everleaf has 3 money flows, and pricing must map to them:

Gift (one-time or recurring) into a fund

Host choice of who pays fees (guest vs host)

Account layer (embedded brokerage account for the recipient; custodial for minors)

Everything else (templates, thank-you, cards) sits on top.

1) Gift fees (what the giver sees at checkout)
Card / Apple Pay / Google Pay (default)

Total fee shown to payer:
2.9% + $0.30 (card processing) + Everleaf platform fee 1.5% (capped at $10)

How it behaves:

Small gifts: behaves like ~4.4% + $0.30

Large gifts: Everleaf fee caps at $10, so effective % drops (processing still applies)

Why this works:

Simple, transparent

Covers payment costs and leaves you margin on most gifts

Cap prevents sticker shock on large card gifts

Bank transfer (ACH) (recommended for $250+)

Total fee shown to payer:
$1.00 bank transfer + Everleaf platform fee 1.0% (capped at $10)

Why this works:

Dramatically cheaper for larger gifts

Lower fraud/chargeback risk vs cards

You still earn platform margin

Recurring gifts (discounted to drive the $5B outcome)

Recurring gifts get a lower Everleaf fee:

Card recurring: Everleaf fee 1.0% (cap $10) instead of 1.5%

ACH recurring: Everleaf fee 0.5% (cap $10) instead of 1.0%

This creates a powerful adoption nudge without changing the core flow.

2) Host plans (what parents or adults pay)
Free

$0 to create

Guests pay standard gift fees at checkout

Unlimited fund links (Always-on Fund)

Up to 3 event pages per fund (Birthday, Bar Mitzvah, Graduation, etc.)

Basic templates + QR

Thank-you export

Seed mode included

This tier is always margin-positive because guests pay fees.

Plus (per event)

$79 per event
Built for weddings and big milestones where hosts want “no fees for guests.”

Includes:

Host pays fees toggle ON (guests see $0 fees)

Premium event templates + matching cards

Auto thank-you drafts

Privacy controls

Keepsake templates (PDF)

Fee rules when Host Pays is enabled:

Host pays processing at cost (card fees or ACH fees)

Everleaf platform fee becomes:

0% Everleaf fee up to $7,500 gift volume per event

0.75% Everleaf fee above $7,500 (still capped at $10 per gift)

Why Plus is priced this way:

Hosts are paying for guest experience + premium workflow

Your downside is capped and controlled

Above the cap you revert to a margin-producing take rate

Family (annual household plan)

$129 per year
For households with multiple kids and multiple events.

Includes:

Up to 10 event pages per year across funds

Host pays toggle included on all events

Household dashboard (multiple kids)

Recurring contribution management

Premium templates and thank-you tools

Fee rules when Host Pays is enabled:

Host pays processing at cost

Everleaf platform fee becomes:

0% Everleaf fee up to $15,000 gift volume per year

0.75% Everleaf fee above $15,000 (capped at $10 per gift)

Why Family is the real business:

You monetize the household relationship

Multi-event LTV

Recurring gifting becomes normal

3) Optional add-ons (high margin, zero confusion)

These should be visible only after the core gift is complete or on the host dashboard.

For givers:

Premium card designs: $2.99

Printed card mailed: $7.99

Framed “Seednote” certificate: $29

For hosts:

Address collection + thank-you send workflow: $9 per event (free in Plus/Family)

Extra event page pack (if on Free): $19 (3 more event pages)

4) B2B pricing (distribution engine)

Organizations get bulk tools and support.

Option A (simple):

$499 per year includes 25 events

Additional events: $15 per event

Gifts follow Free rules unless org enables host-paid

Option B (fee-subsidized):

Org prepays a subsidy pool, and you bill processing at cost plus a negotiated platform fee

5) Embedded brokerage account economics (how you avoid “we pay more than we earn”)

This is the internal reality you price for.

Your costs will include some mix of:

KYC/AML and identity checks per account

Account servicing costs (statements/tax docs)

Trading/clearing costs per trade or per dollar

Program minimums

So Everleaf must guarantee margin from at least one of:

gift take-rate (Free tier does this)

household plan revenue (Family does this)

event plan revenue (Plus does this)

backend brokerage economics (upside, not required to survive)

This pricing model ensures:

Free is always contribution-margin positive

Plus/Family are “fee-free for guests,” but only by shifting cost to host

Caps limit your downside

Above caps, you return to margin-producing fees

7) The one operational rule you must enforce

If a gift is invested (trade executed), refunds are no longer instant. You need a clear rule:

Refundable until trade execution

After execution: reversal is sell + settlement timeline

This reduces chargeback risk and support load.
