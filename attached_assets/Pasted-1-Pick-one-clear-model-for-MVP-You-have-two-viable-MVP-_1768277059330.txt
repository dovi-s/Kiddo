1) Pick one clear model for MVP

You have two viable MVP models. Pick one and make it painfully obvious.

Model A (cleanest MVP): “Gifts invest automatically” is real only after activation

Before activation: guests can “pledge” (or “promise”) gifts. No money moves.

After activation (KYC done): pledges convert to real gifts, money moves, investing happens.

This matches what your screenshots already imply (“pledges convert once you activate investing”).

Model B (simpler conceptually for users): Collect cash first, invest later

Gifts are collected as cash in a holding balance.

Auto-invest happens on a schedule (daily, weekly), once the account is active.

This avoids explaining pledges, but you must clearly show “Cash balance” vs “Invested.”

If you are already built around pledges, stick with Model A.

2) MVP status system (this is the product)

Do not show “Stocks | Bonds | Cash” in MVP. Show statuses.

Use 4 statuses max, with one-line definitions everywhere (tooltips or inline):

Pledged: “They said they’ll give. No payment yet.”

Processing: “Payment submitted. Waiting for bank/card confirmation.”

Received: “Funds received and available to invest.”

Invested: “Funds placed into the portfolio.”

If you only support one asset mix in MVP, “Invested” is enough. Users do not need holdings UI.

3) The only investing surface you need on the dashboard

One “Investing” row, not a tab set.

If activated

“Investing: On”

“Auto-invest: Next market day” (or “next business day”)

“Typical timing: 1 to 2 business days” (match reality)

Link: “How gifts are invested” (simple explainer)

If not activated

A single primary CTA: Activate investing

Two bullets:

“Takes about 2 minutes”

“Identity verification required”

One sentence: “Until activated, people can pledge gifts. No money moves.”

That is it.

4) What “Activate investing” really means in MVP

In MVP, “Activate investing” is not a feature. It is a gate that prevents confusion.

Minimum requirements:

KYC for the adult (guardian), not the child

Link bank account (or whatever funding rail you use)

Accept legal terms (brokerage disclosures)

Do not expose any of the brokerage complexity in the UI beyond:

who custody is with (one line)

SIPC/FINRA mention (one line)

where to read details (link)

5) Guest page copy that prevents 80% of support

On the guest event page, put one compact “What happens” block:

What happens to your gift

“Your gift is received securely.”

“Once the account is active, it is invested automatically for long-term growth.”

“You’ll get a confirmation and receipt.”

If not activated, swap the middle line:

“Right now, gifts are pledges until the account is activated.”

Do not mention tickers, allocations, or returns.

6) Receipts and records: keep it minimal but correct

For each gift entry (owner view):

Name

Amount

Status (from the 4)

Note

Timestamp

“Receipt” link (once processing/received)

For the guest:

Confirmation screen

Email receipt

Avoid showing “portfolio value” as the first number if it includes market moves. In MVP, lead with Total received and show Invested value second.

7) The one thing you must not fake

Do not blur “pending” in a way that implies money is invested when it is not.

If money is not actually at the broker yet, do not show it in “portfolio value.” Put it in “pending/processing.”

8) Practical MVP recommendation for your current screens

Based on what you showed:

Keep “Total received” and “Pending.”

Replace “Stocks | Int’l | Bonds | Cash” with “Pledged | Processing | Received | Invested.”

Keep “Activate investing” exactly as the gate.

Remove projections until users ask for them (or tuck under a small “Details” link).
