About the Company
EarlyBird is a Fintech gifting platform that simplifies investing for parents, while empowering friends and family to seamlessly gift financial contributions to the children in their life. EarlyBird is transforming financial gifting, making it personal, purposeful, and timeless.

Products & Services
EarlyBird Custodial Investment Account (UGMA Account)
A tax-advantaged custodial investment account managed by a custodian for a child until adulthood, allowing family and friends to contribute funds for any purpose.
EarlyBird Mobile App
A mobile application that enables parents to open custodial investment accounts, add digital memories, and invite family and friends to contribute to a child's financial future.
