Geometric, rounded terminals

1 mark + wordmark, nothing else

Three concrete logo concepts (pick one)
Option A: “Ribbon K” (my top pick)

A rounded ribbon stroke that folds into a K. Feels premium and timeless.

Option B: “Monoline K” (most tech)

what does kora mean 

“Kora” has a few real-world meanings and associations:

Kora (Tibetan Buddhism): a ritual walk or circumambulation around a sacred site, done repeatedly as a practice. Association: ritual, return, tradition, life events, “coming back each year.”

Kora (music): a West African 21-string harp-lute (often spelled “kora”). Association: heritage, celebration, storytelling.

Kora (sport/brand/location): it is also used as a name across different countries and products, so it is not a blank slate.

For your business, the strongest meaning is the first one: a recurring life ritual. That fits “always-on family fund” and “every birthday, every milestone” without sounding like finance.

Two cautions:

It does not inherently mean “gifting” or “investing,” so the brand story has to supply that.

Because it’s used elsewhere, you would need trademark/domain checks to see if it’s viable in fintech.
