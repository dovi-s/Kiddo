Yes! And it's easy! The traditional method, which does require the recipient's SSN and other information, is to go to a broker and ask them to set up an account, or to transfer some of your holdings into the recipient's account. There are privacy issues, as social security numbers and brokerage account numbers are needed, and I personally am not comfortable sharing, or asking, for these. Someone could steal my identity!

Luckily, there is a service which makes giving stocks as gifts to friends and family very easy. SparkGift (http://www.sparkgift.com) is a service which makes this process as easy as ordering off of Amazon.

With SparkGift, I simply choose the stock, how many shares I wish to purchase (or how much money I wish to use, they support fractional shares), and then purchase the gift. Then, the recipient sets up their account, and they don't need to share their information with you.

It's clean, simple, and easy. What's not to love?

Yes, you can; but it’s much easier to just buy it in the target account and pay for it with a check you deposit to the target account, rather than go through a broker transfer.

It’s a little different if you ALREADY OWN the stock. It would be foolish to sacrifice LTCG treatment (talking about the US)
