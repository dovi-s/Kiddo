UX principles that make it feel perfected
1) “No decisions unless you ask”

Primary public flow: amount, message, pay.

Anything else is:

an optional secondary step

or behind a drawer

2) The trust loop is visible but not loud

Trust is not a big banner. It is a quiet strip and a drawer.

Trust strip items:

No account required

Apple Pay

Instant receipt

Secure checkout

Then “How it works” drawer with plain language.

3) Always show “what happens next”

Every step shows the next expected thing.

Examples:

“Invests by end of day”

“Held as Seed until guardian invests”

“You can edit your message later”

4) Make gifting feel like sending a premium card

The success state should feel like “I nailed it.”
