Time to sign up for a discover card.
A couple things to note. They are not selling stock...they are basically selling gift cards which get redeemed to a brokerage account. If you don't already have a stockpile brokerage account you have to apply and be approved for one. Then you redeem the gift card and can buy any of the stocks available through their brokerage. So just because you buy $100 gift card for Amazon stock doesn't mean you bought the stock. It's not until you redeem the gift card and process the buy transaction through your stockpile account. Check their fees page. It's free to "switch to another stock", opting out for a retailer gift card", etc. Second...isn't there a gift card exclusion for discover it/apple pay? So would you really be getting 22% back?

One question would be can you redeem the gift card then transfer the money to your bank account for free? Or do they require you to pick a stock or merchant gift card.

If someone wants to invest in a stock this might be worth the effort if you can get a 10%-22% discount. Unfortunately you have a 6% upfront fee when buying the $100 GC's. Also Stockpile has no where near the support/options that other brokers provide (hence the .99 fee for trades).

https://www.stockpile.com/fees
