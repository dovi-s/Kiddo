yes. also consdier the following: Both. Make “new by default,” and “connect existing” as an advanced option.

Default: parents open a new account in your platform

Best for the mainstream gifting UX because it is predictable and instant.

If the recipient is a minor

Parents open a custodial account (UGMA/UTMA-style) and act as custodian until age of majority. These are irrevocable gifts to the child and must be used for the child’s benefit. Fidelity+2Vanguard+2

This becomes your standard “kid gifting” pathway.

If the recipient is 18+

They open their own brokerage account in-product and gifts land there.

Advanced: connect an existing brokerage account

This is for people who already invest and want everything consolidated.

You support 2 connection types:

1) “Transfer in-kind” the holdings (move assets without selling)

Use ACATS/in-kind transfer patterns so the recipient can move their existing positions into the brokerage rail you use, preserving the holdings. FINRA describes the ACATS transfer process starting with a Transfer Initiation Form (TIF). FINRA+2NerdWallet+2

2) “Gift shares” directly to an external brokerage

Some brokerages offer forms and flows specifically for gifting shares to accounts at other firms. Fidelity+1
For certain transfers, “DTC” style broker-to-broker instructions are commonly used (you see this in standard transfer instruction documents). CURE Childhood Cancer+1

In your UX this is: “Already have an account? Send gifts there instead.”

How you present it in the product

On the event setup screen, parents pick one:

Recommended: “Create a new Grow Account for [Child]” (custodial if minor)

“Use my child’s existing brokerage” (connect, verify ownership, then route gifts)

“Let me decide later” (gifts accrue as Seed, auto-invests after a rule)

Why “new by default” wins

Less friction for guests (no “where should I send this?” problem).

Fewer edge-case failures (account mismatches, transfer delays).

Cleaner brand promise: every gift becomes ownership immediately.

Then you add “connect existing” once you have strong traction, because it is valuable but operationally heavier.
